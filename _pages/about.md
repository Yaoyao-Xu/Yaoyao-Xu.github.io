I am a CS PhD student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, where I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Tianshu Yu](https://mypage.cuhk.edu.cn/academics/yutianshu/).

My research focuses on **AI for Science**. I develop learning methods that connect **representation, simulation, and design** for scientific discovery in protein and chemical systems. My work spans protein language models, multiscale protein dynamics, chemical reasoning, and closed-loop molecular design.

Before my PhD, I obtained my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Columbia University, and my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Sichuan University and the University of Pittsburgh.
